BEYOND ORBIT

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

1. Introduction

Beyond Orbit is made by FinPixel Games, a Finnish game studio. This Privacy Policy explains what information Beyond Orbit collects, why, and how you can control it.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Local save data

Beyond Orbit doesn't require an account or sign-up. Your progress (credits, ship/planet unlocks, stats, and achievement progress) is saved as a file on your own device (user://save.json) and never leaves it, except where Google Play Games Services syncs your achievements (see 2.2). Uninstalling the app deletes this data permanently.

2.2 Google Play Games Services

If you're signed in to Play Games on your device, Beyond Orbit syncs your achievement progress to your Google account so it's visible in your Play Games profile. This involves your Play Games account's sign-in state and achievement unlock/progress values. We don't see or store this ourselves. It's held by Google under your own Google account.

2.3 Advertising

Beyond Orbit can show an optional rewarded video ad (Google AdMob) if you choose to watch one for a daily credits bonus. It is never shown automatically, and not at all if you own the premium unlock (section 2.4). Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID and ad-interaction data to serve and measure that ad. On first launch you're shown Google's consent (UMP) prompt to control ad personalization; you can also change this anytime in your device's system settings.

2.4 Purchases

Beyond Orbit offers one optional purchase, a "Remove Ads / Premium" unlock, processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We receive only a purchase confirmation, never your payment card details.

4. Children & Age Rating

Beyond Orbit is rated PEGI 7 / Everyone 10+. We don't collect any personal information directly from any player, of any age. Everything in section 2 is either held locally on your device or handled entirely by Google under your own Google account.

5. Your Rights (GDPR)

We're based in Finland (EU). Since Beyond Orbit doesn't have an account system and holds no personal data of yours ourselves, there's no "delete your account" request to make with us. Instead:

  • Local save data: uninstalling the app deletes it immediately and permanently
  • Play Games data: manage or delete it from your Google Account's Play Games settings
  • Advertising ID / ad personalization: manage it from your device's system settings
  • Purchase history: visible in your Google Play account

If you have any other question about your data, contact us (section 7).

6. Data Retention

Local save data persists on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Play Games, AdMob, and Play Billing data is retained according to Google's own policies, under your Google account.
