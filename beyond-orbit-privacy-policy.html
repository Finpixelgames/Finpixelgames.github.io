BEYOND ORBIT

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

1. Introduction

Beyond Orbit is made by FinPixel Games, a Finnish game studio. This Privacy Policy explains what information Beyond Orbit collects, why, and how you can control it.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Local save data

Beyond Orbit doesn't require an account or sign-up. Your progress (credits, ship/planet unlocks, stats, and achievement progress) is saved as a file on your own device (user://save.json) and never leaves it, except where Google Play Games Services syncs your achievements (see 2.2). Uninstalling the app deletes this data permanently.

2.2 Google Play Games Services

If you're signed in to Play Games on your device, Beyond Orbit syncs your achievement progress to your Google account so it's visible in your Play Games profile. This involves your Play Games account's sign-in state and achievement unlock/progress values. We don't see or store this ourselves. It's held by Google under your own Google account.

2.3 Advertising

Beyond Orbit can show an optional rewarded video ad (Google AdMob) if you choose to watch one for a daily credits bonus or to revive your ship after a crash. It is never shown automatically, and not at all if you own the premium unlock (section 2.4). Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID and ad-interaction data to serve and measure that ad. On first launch you're shown Google's consent (UMP) prompt to control ad personalization; you can also change this anytime in your device's system settings.

2.4 Purchases

Beyond Orbit offers one optional purchase, a "Remove Ads / Premium" unlock, processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We receive only a purchase confirmation, never your payment card details.

2.5 Anonymous usage statistics

To find out where the game is too hard, too slow or confusing, Beyond Orbit sends a small amount of gameplay statistics to our own server. This helps us fix crashes, slow loading on older phones, and difficulty spikes.

  • What is sent: a random install ID created by the game (not your advertising ID or any account), game events such as starting the app, starting and ending a flight (length, distance, cause, level reached, frame rate), upgrades bought, missions, achievements, rewarded ads watched and purchases made, plus technical info: device model, graphics chip, Android version, screen size, memory, language and game version.
  • What is not sent: your name, email, Google account, advertising ID, location, contacts or any other personal content.
  • How to turn it off: Settings → Usage statistics. When you switch it off, nothing more is sent and any unsent statistics on your device are deleted.

The statistics are pseudonymous: we can't link the install ID to you as a person. We use them only to improve the game. We never sell them or share them with advertisers.

2.6 Squadrons (optional)

Squadrons lets you team up with other players for weekly operations and rankings. It is completely optional: nothing is sent until you open Squadron in the main menu and choose a pilot name.

  • What is stored on our server: a random pilot ID and a secret key created by the game (we keep only a scrambled fingerprint of the key), the pilot name you choose plus a short tag such as #K4R7, your pilot rank and ship, flight results that count toward squadron points (pirates, bosses, planets and asteroids destroyed, best distance), the squadron you create or join, rewards you claim, and reports you send about other pilots or squadrons.
  • Who can see it: other players can see your pilot name, tag, rank, squadron and squadron stats in the Squadron screens and rankings. Don't use your real name. Pilot and squadron names are filtered for offensive words, and players can report names.
  • What is not collected: no email, Google account, advertising ID, location, chat messages or contacts. There is no chat.
  • How to delete it: Settings → Delete squadron profile removes your pilot, points and squadron membership from our server immediately.

2.7 Pilot wrecks

When your ship is destroyed, its wreck can appear in other players' space at the same flight level, and they can salvage it for a small reward. You get a note in the menu when pilots salvage your wreck.

  • What is sent: when your flight ends in a crash, the random pilot ID created by the game and a scrambled fingerprint of its secret key, the flight level, the distance flown and your ship type. When you salvage someone else's wreck, your random pilot ID and the wreck's number.
  • Who can see it: other players see the wreck with your Squadron pilot name if you have one (section 2.6), otherwise only an anonymous callsign such as "Pilot 7F3A". They never see your pilot ID.
  • How to turn it off: switching off Settings → Usage statistics also stops sending wrecks and salvages.
  • How long it is kept: wrecks and salvage records are deleted automatically after 7 days. Delete squadron profile also deletes your wrecks immediately.

4. Children & Age Rating

Beyond Orbit is rated PEGI 7 / Everyone 10+. We don't collect contact details or account information from any player, of any age. The data we receive ourselves is the pseudonymous usage statistics in section 2.5 and pilot wrecks in section 2.7, which can be switched off in Settings, and, only if the player chooses to use Squadrons, the pilot name and flight results in section 2.6. Pilot names are visible to other players, so we recommend a nickname rather than a real name.

5. Your Rights (GDPR)

We're based in Finland (EU). Beyond Orbit doesn't have an account system, so there's no "delete your account" request to make with us. Instead:

  • Squadrons: Settings → Delete squadron profile deletes your pilot and all its points and memberships from our server
  • Usage statistics: switch them off anytime in Settings → Usage statistics. Because they are tied only to a random install ID, we can't look up "your" data by name or email, and uninstalling the app permanently cuts the link between you and that ID
  • Local save data: uninstalling the app deletes it immediately and permanently
  • Play Games data: manage or delete it from your Google Account's Play Games settings
  • Advertising ID / ad personalization: manage it from your device's system settings
  • Purchase history: visible in your Google Play account

If you have any other question about your data, contact us (section 7).

6. Data Retention

Local save data persists on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Usage statistics (section 2.5) are kept for at most 14 months and then deleted. A Squadrons pilot (section 2.6) is deleted when you delete the squadron profile, or automatically after 14 months without opening the game; weekly squadron results older than 14 months are deleted too. Pilot wrecks and salvage records (section 2.7) are deleted after 7 days. Play Games, AdMob, and Play Billing data is retained according to Google's own policies, under your Google account.
