Delete Your Online Profile

Last updated: September 28, 2026

SANDROX creates an online profile only for friends and gifts. You can delete it and all of its data (nickname and #tag, profile look, friend list, friend requests and gifts) at any time. Your game progress on your phone is not affected.

Option 1: In the game (instant)

  1. Open SANDROX
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Tap Delete online profile and confirm

This deletes your online profile and all of its data immediately and permanently.

Option 2: By email (if you can't access the game)

Email: finpixelgames@gmail.com

Please include your friend code (nickname and #tag, for example GoldenShell#A99S) so we can find the right profile.

We process email deletion requests within 30 days.

This is permanent. Your friends and pending gifts are removed and cannot be restored. If you open Friends again later, the game creates a brand new online profile.

For more detail, see the SANDROX Privacy Policy.
