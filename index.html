About

FinPixel is a one-person game studio in Finland.

I studied business, not programming. During an internship I learned to use AI as a work tool, and it let me start doing what I had wanted to do for years, which is making games.

I'm open about how it works. AI writes much of the code with me, helps create the art and makes the music. I decide what the games are, how they feel and when they're good enough to release. People close to me play everything first and tell me honestly what doesn't work.

I only make games I want to play myself.
