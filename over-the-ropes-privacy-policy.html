OVER THE ROPES (ROBLOX)

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. Introduction

Over the Ropes is a game on Roblox made by FinPixel Games, a Finnish game studio. This Privacy Policy explains what information Over the Ropes itself handles, why, and how you can control it.

You play Over the Ropes with your Roblox account. Your account, your friends, chat, Robux and everything else Roblox runs is covered by Roblox's own Privacy Policy, not by this page.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Your game progress

So that your progress is still there the next time you play, the game saves it in Roblox's own storage (Roblox Data Stores), linked to your Roblox user ID:

  • your tickets, the gear and show items you own, your three saved outfits and which one you're wearing
  • matches played and matches won, and the day you last got the daily ticket bonus
  • your music on/off setting
  • a random statistics ID (see section 2.5)

This data stays inside Roblox. We use it only to run the game.

2.2 What other players see

Like in most Roblox games, other players in the same game can see your Roblox display name and avatar, including the outfit you've picked in Over the Ropes. In addition:

  • Top Eliminators board: the game saves your total number of eliminations, linked to your Roblox user ID. The board in the backstage lounge shows the top players' display names and avatar pictures, which the game fetches from Roblox.
  • Recent Champions: the VIP Skybox shows the last match winners' names and avatar pictures. This list exists only on that game server and is not saved.

2.3 Chat

Over the Ropes uses Roblox's built-in chat, which Roblox filters and moderates. The game doesn't read, save or send your chat messages anywhere.

2.4 Purchases

Tickets are earned by playing and spent in the in-game shop. They are not real money and can't be cashed out. Over the Ropes may offer optional Robux purchases, such as ticket packs or passes. All Robux purchases are processed entirely by Roblox. We only learn that the purchase happened, so the game can give you the item. The game has no random paid rewards (no loot boxes): you always see exactly what you get before buying.

2.5 Anonymous gameplay statistics

To find out what's fun, what's confusing and whether the game runs smoothly on phones, Over the Ropes sends a small amount of gameplay statistics to our own server. Only the Roblox game server sends them; your device never connects to our server.

  • What is sent: a random statistics ID created by the game the first time you play (it is not your Roblox user ID and isn't calculated from it), a random ID for each play session, and game events: joining the game, finding the ready zone, starting and finishing a match (your placing, eliminations, tickets earned, how long it took), items bought with tickets, achievements unlocked, using the VIP Skybox, and leaving the game (play time, where the time was spent, how often you used emotes, poses, the shop and your locker). Plus technical info: device type (phone, tablet, computer or console), screen size, frame rate, your language setting (for example en-us), the game version and short error messages from the game server.
  • What is not sent: your Roblox username, display name or user ID, chat, avatar, friends, email, location, IP address or any other personal content.

The statistics are pseudonymous: we can't tell from them who you are. We use them only to improve the game. We never sell them or share them with advertisers.

The game also reports a few gameplay steps (for example reaching your first match) and ticket earnings and spending to Roblox's own analytics for creators. Roblox shows these to us only as totals and charts.

2.6 What we don't collect

Over the Ropes doesn't show ads and doesn't collect your name, email, phone number, location, photos, contacts or anything else outside the game.

4. Children

Over the Ropes is made to be fun and safe for players of all ages, including children. We don't collect any personal information directly from any player. The only data we receive ourselves is the pseudonymous statistics in section 2.5, which contain no name, account or contact details. There are no ads and no random paid rewards. Parents can manage chat, spending and who their child plays with in Roblox's Parental Controls (in the Roblox account settings).

5. Your Rights (GDPR)

We're based in Finland (EU). You can:

  • Delete your game progress and leaderboard entry: email us your Roblox username (section 7), or ask Roblox to delete your Roblox account. When Roblox tells us an account has asked for its data to be deleted, we delete that player's saved progress and Top Eliminators entry.
  • Statistics: they're linked only to the random statistics ID. Once your saved progress is deleted, nothing links that ID to you anymore, so we can't look up "your" statistics by name.
  • Roblox account data (profile, chat, purchases): manage or delete it through Roblox.

If you have any other question about your data, contact us (section 7).

6. Data Retention

Your game progress and eliminations are kept as long as Over the Ropes exists, so you can come back to them, unless you ask us to delete them (section 5). Gameplay statistics (section 2.5) are kept for at most 14 months and then deleted automatically. Roblox keeps its own data according to its own policies.
