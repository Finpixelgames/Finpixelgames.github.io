YOUR HOCKEY CAREER

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

1. Introduction

Your Hockey Career is made by FinPixel Games, a Finnish game studio. This Privacy Policy explains what information Your Hockey Career collects, why, and how you can control it.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Local save data

Your Hockey Career doesn't require an account or sign-up. Your career (your player's name, attributes, stats, contracts, money and items), your settings and your purchase status are saved as files on your own device and never leave it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes this data permanently.

2.2 Google Play Games Services

If you're signed in to Play Games on your device, Your Hockey Career uses it for leaderboards (for example career goals, assists, points, games played and championships) and achievements. This involves your Play Games account's sign-in state, your Play Games gamer name and avatar, your scores and your achievement progress. Your gamer name, avatar and scores are shown to other players on the leaderboards. We don't see or store this ourselves. It's held by Google under your own Google account, and you can control your Play Games profile visibility in your Play Games settings.

2.3 Advertising

Your Hockey Career shows ads from Google AdMob. Rewarded video ads are always optional: you choose to watch one for an in-game reward, such as extra energy or double match pay. A full-screen ad may also be shown between matches, at most after every third match and never during the first matches of a new career. No ads are shown between matches if you own the Remove Ads purchase (section 2.4).

Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads. On first launch you're shown Google's consent (UMP) prompt to control ad personalization; you can also change this anytime in your device's system settings.

2.4 Purchases

Your Hockey Career offers optional purchases: a Remove Ads unlock and packs of in-game money. All purchases are processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We receive only a purchase confirmation, never your payment card details.

2.5 Reviews and updates

Your Hockey Career may occasionally ask you to rate the game using Google Play's in-app review prompt, and may tell you when a new version is available using Google Play's in-app updates. Both are handled by Google Play. We never see your review details through the game, only the public reviews on Google Play.

2.6 Anonymous usage statistics

To find out where the game is too hard, too slow or confusing, Your Hockey Career sends a small amount of gameplay statistics to our own server. This helps us fix crashes, slow performance on older phones, and balance problems.

  • What is sent: a random install ID created by the game (not your advertising ID or any account), game events such as starting the app, starting and finishing a match (score, your stats, time on ice), career progress (season, league, contracts), training, rewarded ads watched and purchases made, plus technical info: device model, graphics chip, Android version, screen size, memory, language and game version.
  • What is not sent: your name, your player's name, email, Google account, advertising ID, location, contacts or any other personal content.
  • How to turn it off: Settings → Usage statistics. When you switch it off, nothing more is sent and any unsent statistics on your device are deleted.

The statistics are pseudonymous: we can't link the install ID to you as a person. We use them only to improve the game. We never sell them or share them with advertisers.

2.7 What we don't collect

Apart from the usage statistics above, we don't collect anything ourselves. We don't collect your name, email, Google account, location, contacts or any other personal content.

4. Children & Age Rating

Your Hockey Career is intended for players aged 13 and older. We don't collect any personal information directly from any player, of any age. The only data we receive ourselves is the pseudonymous usage statistics in section 2.6, which contain no name, account or contact details and can be switched off in Settings.

5. Your Rights (GDPR)

We're based in Finland (EU). Your Hockey Career doesn't have an account system, so there's no "delete your account" request to make with us. Instead:

  • Usage statistics: switch them off anytime in Settings → Usage statistics. Because they are tied only to a random install ID, we can't look up "your" data by name or email, and uninstalling the app permanently cuts the link between you and that ID
  • Local save data: uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it immediately and permanently
  • Play Games data (leaderboard scores, achievements, profile visibility): manage or delete it from your Google Account's Play Games settings
  • Advertising ID / ad personalization: manage it from your device's system settings
  • Purchase history: visible in your Google Play account

If you have any other question about your data, contact us (section 7).

6. Data Retention

Local save data persists on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Usage statistics (section 2.6) are kept for at most 14 months and then deleted. Play Games, AdMob and Play Billing data is retained according to Google's own policies, under your Google account.
