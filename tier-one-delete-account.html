Delete Your Account

Last updated: August 2, 2026

You can permanently delete your TIER ONE account and all associated data (profile, cards, chat history, friends, faction membership, and match history) at any time. There are two ways to do this:

Option 1 — In the app (instant)

  1. Open TIER ONE and log in
  2. Go to your profile icon → Settings
  3. Scroll to the Account section
  4. Tap Delete Account and confirm

This deletes your account and all of its data immediately and permanently. This cannot be undone.

Option 2 — By email (if you can't access the app)

If you've lost access to your account or device, email us and we'll delete it for you:

Email: finpixelgames@gmail.com

Please include the username and, if possible, the email address your account was registered with, so we can verify and locate the right account.

We process email deletion requests within 30 days.

This is permanent. Deleting your account removes your cards, coins, rating, chat history, friends, and faction membership immediately, and cannot be reversed. You're free to sign up again afterward with the same or a different email, as a brand-new account.

For more detail on what data is deleted and what we store while your account exists, see the TIER ONE Privacy Policy.
