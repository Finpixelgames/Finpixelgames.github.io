Pocket Brawlers

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. Introduction

Pocket Brawlers is made by FinPixel Games, a Finnish game studio. This Privacy Policy explains what information Pocket Brawlers collects, why, and how you can control it.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Local save data

Pocket Brawlers doesn't require an account or sign-up. Your progress (coins, teeth and gold teeth, your tenants and their skills, floors, rooms, items and pets, tasks, settings, and your in-game nickname, profile picture and house name) is saved as a file on your own device (user://save.json). So that you can continue after reinstalling the game or on a new phone, the save file is also included in your device's Android backup, if backup is turned on in your device settings. Uninstalling the app deletes the file from your device.

2.2 House Call: your online house and visits

So that other players can visit your house and you can visit theirs, Pocket Brawlers puts your house online when you play. No email, password or sign-up is needed: the game gets a random account ID automatically, and the login for it is kept with your save data on your device.

The online house stores your nickname and house name, the number of floors and tenants, a snapshot of your house (room types and wallpapers, the position of your items, and your strongest tenants with their skill levels), how many times your house has been visited, and when it was last updated. It also stores the visits between houses (who visited whom, when, and the teeth the host earned; visits are deleted after 90 days), the houses you have added to your own friends list, houses you have blocked, and reports you have made about offensive names.

What other players see: your nickname, house name, floor count, visit count and the snapshot of your house, in the TOP and RECENT lists, in search results by name and when they visit you. After a visit, the host sees the visitor's nickname and house name in their mailbox. There is no chat and no free text other than the nickname and house name, which are checked for inappropriate words. You can block or report any house you visit. A house reported by three different players is hidden from the lists and its names are reset to defaults.

This data is stored with Supabase in the EU (Stockholm, Sweden) and is used only to run House Call visits. It is never sold or used for advertising.

You can take your house offline at any time in the game: Settings → Delete my online house. This deletes your online house, your visit log, your friends list, your blocks and your reports right away. Your house only goes online again if you choose Put my house online.

2.3 Advertising and purchases

The current version of Pocket Brawlers shows no ads and offers no real-money purchases. If they are added later, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

2.4 Anonymous usage statistics

To find out where the game is too hard, too slow or confusing, Pocket Brawlers sends a small amount of gameplay statistics to our own server. This helps us fix slow performance on older phones, improve the tutorial and balance the economy.

  • What is sent: a random install ID created by the game (not your advertising ID or online house account ID), game events such as starting the app, tutorial steps, hiring tenants, building floors, upgrades, shop purchases with in-game currency, tasks and daily gifts claimed, house events and visits started and finished, plus technical info: device model, graphics chip, number of processor cores, operating system and its version, screen size, language and game version.
  • What is not sent: your nickname, house name, the houses you visit, email, Google account, advertising ID, location, contacts or any other personal content.
  • How to turn it off: Settings → Usage stats. When you switch it off, nothing more is sent.

The statistics are pseudonymous: we can't link the install ID to you as a person, and they are kept separate from the online house in section 2.2. We use them only to improve the game. We never sell them or share them with advertisers.

4. Children

Pocket Brawlers is a general audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. The online house (section 2.2) contains only a nickname and house name chosen by the player and no contact details, and there is no chat.

5. Your Rights (GDPR)

We're based in Finland (EU). You can access, correct and delete your data:

  • Online house: change your nickname and house name in the game, block or report houses, or delete your online house with its visits, friends list and blocks in Settings → Delete my online house (instant), or by email (section 7)
  • Local save data: uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it from your device; the copy in your Android backup can be deleted in your Google Account settings
  • Usage statistics: switch them off anytime in Settings → Usage stats. Because they are tied only to a random install ID, we can't look up "your" statistics by name or email, and uninstalling the app permanently cuts the link between you and that ID

If you have any other question about your data, contact us (section 7).

6. Data Retention

Local save data stays on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data. The online house, its friends list, blocks and reports are kept until you delete it; visits are deleted after 90 days. Usage statistics (section 2.4) are deleted automatically after 14 months.
