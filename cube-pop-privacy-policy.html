Cube Pop

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Cube Pop is made by FinPixel Games, a Finnish game studio. This Privacy Policy explains what information Cube Pop collects, why, and how you can control it.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Local save data

Cube Pop doesn't require an account or sign-up. Your progress (levels, coins, boosters, lives, cards, event and pass progress, settings, and your in-game name, profile picture and frame) is saved as a file on your own device (user://save.json), and the purchases you own (section 2.4) are recorded in a separate file next to it. If you're signed in to Play Games, a copy of your save file is stored in Play Games saved games (section 2.5). We never receive these copies ourselves. Uninstalling the app deletes the files from your device.

2.2 Teams, team chat and leaderboards

So that you can join a team, chat with your teammates, trade cards and appear on the team and country leaderboards, Cube Pop keeps a small online profile for you. No email, password or sign-up is needed: the game gets a random account ID automatically, and the login for it is kept on your device. A recovery key for this account is also stored in your Play Games saved games, so that your team membership can follow you to a new device.

The online profile stores your in-game name, profile picture and frame choice, your level, your country (taken from your phone's language setting, for example "FI"), your team and role, your weekly team points, the card requests you have made and the cards you have sent, the players you have blocked and the reports you have made. A team stores its name, description, crest and member list.

Team chat: messages you write in team chat are visible to the members of your team. Messages are filtered for inappropriate words, and you can report any message or block any player from the message itself. A message reported by three different players is hidden from everyone. Chat messages are deleted automatically after 30 days.

What other players see: your name, profile picture, frame and level in your team's member list, in team chat and on the country leaderboard; team names, crests and points in the team list and team leaderboard.

This data is stored with Supabase in the EU (Stockholm, Sweden) and is used only to run teams, chat and leaderboards. It is never sold or used for advertising. You can delete it at any time in the game: Settings → Delete my online data. This removes your online profile, your team membership and your chat messages right away; your progress on your device stays.

2.3 Anonymous usage statistics

To find out where the game is too hard, too slow or confusing, Cube Pop sends a small amount of gameplay statistics to our own server. This helps us balance the levels, fix slow performance on older phones and improve the tutorial.

  • What is sent: a random install ID created by the game (not your advertising ID or online account ID), game events such as starting the app, starting and finishing levels (result, duration and how many cubes were left), boosters and continues used, ads watched, real-money purchases made, whether you restored your progress from Play Games saved games, and session length, plus technical info: device model, graphics chip, number of processor cores, memory, operating system and its version, screen size, language and game version.
  • What is not sent: your name, team, chat messages, email, Google account, advertising ID, location, contacts or any other personal content.
  • How to turn it off: Settings → Usage stats. When you switch it off, nothing more is sent.

The statistics are pseudonymous and kept separate from the online profile in section 2.2. We use them only to improve the game and never sell them or share them with advertisers.

2.4 Advertising and purchases

Ads: Cube Pop shows ads from Google AdMob. Rewarded video ads are always optional: you choose to watch one for a reward, such as an extra slot to continue a level, double coins or an extra life. From level 15, a full-screen ad may be shown between levels. No full-screen ads are shown if you own the No Ads purchase.

Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads. On first launch you're shown Google's consent (UMP) prompt to control ad personalization; you can also change this anytime in your device's system settings.

Purchases: Cube Pop offers optional purchases: coin packs, a booster bundle, Pop Pass Premium and a one-time No Ads purchase. All purchases are processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We receive only a purchase confirmation, never your payment card details.

2.5 Google Play Games Services

If you're signed in to Play Games on your device, Cube Pop uses it for:

  • Cloud save: a copy of your save file (section 2.1) and your online recovery key (section 2.2) are stored in Play Games saved games under your own Google account, with a short summary (level, cubes popped, coins and the time it was saved). On a new device, after a reinstall or on PC, the game can offer to restore your progress. We don't see or store this copy ourselves.
  • Leaderboards: your highest level, cubes popped and Bubble Rush points are submitted to Play Games leaderboards, where other players can see your Play Games name and score according to your Play Games profile settings.
  • Achievements: achievements you unlock are recorded in your Play Games profile.

You can sign in, see achievements and leaderboards in Settings → Google Play Games, and manage your Play Games profile and saved games in your Google Account settings.

4. Children

Cube Pop is a general audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. The online profile (section 2.2) contains only a name chosen by the player and no contact details; team chat is filtered and every message can be reported or its sender blocked.

5. Your Rights (GDPR)

We're based in Finland (EU). You can access, correct and delete your data:

  • Online profile, team and chat: change your name and picture in the game, leave your team, block or report players, or delete your online data in Settings → Delete my online data (instant), or by email (section 7)
  • Local save data: uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it from your device; the copy in Play Games saved games can be deleted in your Google Account settings
  • Advertising ID / ad personalization: manage it from your device's system settings
  • Purchase history: visible in your Google Play account
  • Usage statistics: switch them off anytime in Settings → Usage stats. Because they are tied only to a random install ID, we can't look up "your" statistics by name or email, and uninstalling the app permanently cuts the link between you and that ID

If you have any other question about your data, contact us (section 7).

6. Data Retention

Local save data stays on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Its copy in Play Games saved games is kept under your Google account until you delete it. Play Games, AdMob and Play Billing data is retained according to Google's own policies. Team chat messages and card requests are deleted after 30 days and reports after 90 days. Your online profile is kept until you delete it, or deleted automatically if the game hasn't connected for 14 months. Usage statistics (section 2.3) are deleted automatically after 14 months.
