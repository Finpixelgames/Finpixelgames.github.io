SANDROX

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

1. Introduction

SANDROX is made by FinPixel Games, a Finnish game studio. This Privacy Policy explains what information SANDROX collects, why, and how you can control it.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Local save data

SANDROX doesn't require an account or sign-up. Your progress (coins, current shift and sandbox, unlocked items, goals, settings, and your in-game nickname and profile look) is saved as a file on your own device (user://save.json) and never leaves it. Your nickname is not sent to us or shown to other players. Uninstalling the app deletes this data permanently.

2.2 Notifications

If you allow notifications, SANDROX schedules reminders (for example, that your daily gift is ready) locally on your device. No push service or server is involved, and you can turn reminders off in the game's settings or in your device's system settings at any time.

2.3 Advertising

SANDROX shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch (for example, to double a reward), and occasional ads between shifts. Ads between shifts are not shown if you buy "Remove Ads". Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, device information and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads. In the EU and other regions where it is required, you're shown Google's consent (UMP) prompt to control ad personalization. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's system settings.

2.4 Purchases

SANDROX offers optional in-app purchases (coin packs, a starter pack, Remove Ads and cosmetic items), processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We receive only a purchase confirmation, never your payment card details.

4. Children

SANDROX is a general audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children, and we don't collect any personal information directly from any player. Everything in section 2 is either stored locally on your device or handled by Google.

5. Your Rights (GDPR)

We're based in Finland (EU). Since SANDROX has no account system and we hold no personal data of yours ourselves, there's no account to delete with us. Instead:

  • Local save data: uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it immediately and permanently
  • Advertising ID and ad personalization: manage them from your device's system settings
  • Notifications: turn them off in the game's settings or your device's system settings
  • Purchase history: visible in your Google Play account

If you have any other question about your data, contact us (section 7).

6. Data Retention

Local save data stays on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data. AdMob and Play Billing data is retained according to Google's own policies.
