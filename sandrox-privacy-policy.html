SANDROX

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

1. Introduction

SANDROX is made by FinPixel Games, a Finnish game studio. This Privacy Policy explains what information SANDROX collects, why, and how you can control it.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Local save data

SANDROX doesn't require an account or sign-up. Your progress (coins, current shift and sandbox, unlocked items, goals, settings, and your in-game nickname and profile look) is saved as a file on your own device (user://save.json). Your nickname and profile look stay on your device unless you open Friends, which creates the optional online profile described in section 2.2. Uninstalling the app deletes the save file permanently.

2.2 Friends and gifts (online profile)

SANDROX creates an online profile only when you open Friends for the first time. No email, password or sign-up is needed: the game gets a random account ID automatically. The online profile stores your nickname and #tag, the look of your profile (avatar, frame, name color, title and card background), your friend list and friend requests, gifts sent between friends, and when you last opened Friends. Other players can see your nickname, #tag and profile look only when they know your friend code or are your friends. Nicknames are checked on the server for inappropriate words. This data is stored with Supabase in the EU (Stockholm, Sweden) and is used only to run friends and gifts. It is never sold or used for advertising.

You can delete your online profile anytime in the game: Settings → Delete online profile. See also how to delete your online profile.

2.3 Notifications

If you allow notifications, SANDROX schedules reminders (for example, that your daily gift is ready) locally on your device. No push service or server is involved, and you can turn reminders off in the game's settings or in your device's system settings at any time.

2.4 Advertising

SANDROX shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch (for example, to double a reward), and occasional ads between shifts. Ads between shifts are not shown if you buy "Remove Ads". Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, device information and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads. In the EU and other regions where it is required, you're shown Google's consent (UMP) prompt to control ad personalization. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's system settings.

2.5 Purchases

SANDROX offers optional in-app purchases (coin packs, a starter pack, Remove Ads and cosmetic items), processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We receive only a purchase confirmation, never your payment card details.

2.6 Anonymous usage statistics

To find out where the game is too hard, too slow or confusing, SANDROX sends a small amount of gameplay statistics to our own server. This helps us fix slow performance on older phones, improve the tutorial and balance orders, tools and rewards.

  • What is sent: a random install ID created by the game (not your advertising ID, account ID or friend code), game events such as starting the app, tutorial steps, starting and ending a shift, bottles shipped or rejected (match %, reward), tools used, daily gifts and goals claimed, coin shop purchases, rewarded and between-shift ads shown, and in-app purchases made, plus technical info: device model, graphics chip, Android version, screen size, memory, language, frame rate and game version.
  • What is not sent: your nickname, #tag, friend list, email, Google account, advertising ID, location, contacts or any other personal content.
  • How to turn it off: Settings → Usage stats. When you switch it off, nothing more is sent and any unsent statistics on your device are deleted.

The statistics are pseudonymous: we can't link the install ID to you as a person, and they are kept separate from the online profile in section 2.2. We use them only to improve the game. We never sell them or share them with advertisers.

4. Children

SANDROX is a general audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. The optional online profile (section 2.2) contains only a nickname chosen by the player and no contact details.

5. Your Rights (GDPR)

We're based in Finland (EU). You can access, correct and delete your data:

  • Online profile (friends and gifts): change your nickname and profile look in the game, or delete the whole online profile in Settings → Delete online profile (instant), or by email (section 7)
  • Local save data: uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it immediately and permanently
  • Advertising ID and ad personalization: manage them from your device's system settings
  • Notifications: turn them off in the game's settings or your device's system settings
  • Purchase history: visible in your Google Play account
  • Usage statistics: switch them off anytime in Settings → Usage stats. Because they are tied only to a random install ID, we can't look up "your" statistics by name or email, and uninstalling the app permanently cuts the link between you and that ID

If you have any other question about your data, contact us (section 7).

6. Data Retention

Local save data stays on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data. The online profile is kept until you delete it; unclaimed gifts are deleted after 7 days. Usage statistics (section 2.6) are deleted automatically after 14 months. AdMob and Play Billing data is retained according to Google's own policies.
